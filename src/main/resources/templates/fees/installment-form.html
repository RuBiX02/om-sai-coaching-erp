A plan only schedules the agreed fee — it never changes the gross fee, the discount or the net fee. Once payments have been booked against the schedule, it can no longer be changed; the payments and their receipts are the history that matters. A schedule can be replaced as long as no payment has been booked against it. After that the schedule is part of the financial record and is kept exactly as it was.
